Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

GuestStar ("we", "our", or "us") operates the platform available at mygueststar.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our service.

We never store your passwords. Authentication is handled exclusively through official Google and Meta servers via OAuth 2.0. Your login credentials never pass through our systems.

1. Information We Collect

When you use GuestStar, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information: your name, email address, and business name provided during registration.
  • OAuth tokens: access tokens provided by Google and Meta after you authorize the connection. These are used solely to read public review data on your behalf.
  • Review data: public ratings and reviews from your Google Business Profile and Facebook Page that you choose to sync.
  • Usage data: information about how you interact with our platform, such as pages visited and features used.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect exclusively to:

  • Provide and operate the GuestStar review management service.
  • Sync and display your public business reviews in one unified dashboard.
  • Generate AI-assisted response suggestions for your reviews.
  • Send you service-related notifications and support communications.
  • Improve and maintain the platform.

3. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect or store your Google or Facebook passwords.
  • We do not access private messages, personal contacts, or any data beyond public review information.
  • We do not collect payment card details directly — all payments are processed securely through Stripe.

4. Google and Meta Data Usage

GuestStar accesses your Google Business Profile and Facebook Page data only after you explicitly authorize the connection. We use this access to:

  • Read public customer reviews and ratings associated with your business.
  • Allow you to draft and publish responses to reviews through the platform.

We do not use your Google or Meta data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond providing the core service described above. Our use of Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: trusted partners such as Stripe (payment processing) and Anthropic (AI response generation) who assist in delivering the service.
  • Legal requirements: if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account information and synced review data for as long as your account is active. If you cancel your account, your data will be deleted within 30 days of the cancellation date.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including SSL/TLS encryption for all data in transit and secure storage for all data at rest. Our platform is hosted on Railway, which provides enterprise-grade infrastructure security.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Revoke OAuth access to your Google or Facebook account at any time through your Google or Meta account settings.

9. Cookies

GuestStar uses session cookies necessary for authentication and to maintain your logged-in state. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated date.
